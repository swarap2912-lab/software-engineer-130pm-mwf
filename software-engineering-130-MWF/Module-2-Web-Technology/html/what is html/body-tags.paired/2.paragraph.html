Diwali, also known as the Festival of Lights, is one of the most popular and joyous festivals celebrated in India and around the world. The festival symbolizes the spiritual victory of light over darkness and good over evil, traditionally commemorating Lord Rama's return to Ayodhya after 14 years in exile. During the five-day celebration, families clean and decorate their homes with colorful rangoli designs, oil lamps (diyas), and candles. People wear new clothes, offer prayers to Goddess Lakshmi for wealth and prosperity, share sweets, and exchange gifts with loved ones.
